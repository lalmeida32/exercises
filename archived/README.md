# Archived

In this repository first conception, the idea was to separate exercises into Computer Science subjects and put the exercises related to each subject.

The problem is: most of the time exercises were not mutually exclusive in terms of Computer Science subjects. Soon it becomes difficult to categorize a specific exercise.

So now the exercises will be categorized by the date of its conception. Because old exercises doesn't have a clear date of conception, we are putting them inside this `archived/` folder.
